Make a Wish



Make a Wish is an Android app for the moments when the clock shows 11:11, 2:22 or 3:33 and you think you should probably make a wish. It sends a small notification at those times, a nudge to pause, take a breath and set an intention, and gives you a place to write that intention down.

You choose when it can bother you: a daytime window and an evening window, each with its own switch, and a bedtime cutoff so that it stays quiet while you sleep. Everyone needs a little reminder sometimes.

In the app itself it currently goes by the name Angel Hours.


Where it is now

Make a Wish is a work in progress, written in Kotlin with Jetpack Compose, and it is not on Google Play yet. The source is open under the GNU GPL v2 on GitHub.
