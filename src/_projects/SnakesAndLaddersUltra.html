Snakes & Ladders: ULTRA



Snakes & Ladders: ULTRA is a hard mode mod for the classic board game Snakes and Ladders (also known as Chutes and Ladders, and based on the ancient Indian game Moksha Patam). You play with a normal board and a normal die, and change four rules.


The rules

Sliding. If your move ends on any part of a snake, not just its head, you slide to the end of the slippery creature.

Climbing. If you finish on any part of a ladder, not just its foot, you climb from that point to the top.

Feats of skill. If you land on a square where a snake and a ladder cross, you have to roll to hold on. Roll a d6: on a 1, 2 or 3 you slip, and on a 4, 5 or 6 you climb.

The final touch. To win you need to roll the exact number to reach the last square. You can't roll over it, you simply don't move, so you could be stuck there for a while as the other players catch up.


Try to beat it, if you dare.
