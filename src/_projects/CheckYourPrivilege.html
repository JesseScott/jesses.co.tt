Check Your Privilege



Check Your Privilege is an Android app that asks you a series of yes-or-no questions about the social advantages and disadvantages you have, and then shows you your answers together. It is meant as an educational tool: a prompt for reflecting on where you stand, not a verdict on you.

You can retake the questionnaire whenever you like.


Where it is now

Check Your Privilege is at an early stage, written in Kotlin, and it is not on Google Play. The source is open under the MIT license on GitHub.
