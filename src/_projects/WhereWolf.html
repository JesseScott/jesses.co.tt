Where Wolf?



Where Wolf? is a small solo or duet tabletop RPG about being in the wrong place at the wrong time. You are a werewolf, an alpha predator who feasts on flesh and bone, but on the full moon you turn into a human. You are far from socialized: you struggle to dress yourself, even to speak. Yet you are strangely drawn to the villages of people you can slip into in this shape, and what you really long for is the gentle, willing, nonviolent touch of another. You just don't know how to get it.


How it plays

You feel the shift coming all day, and skill checks made in that time are made with disadvantage. The shift itself is excruciating, messy and loud, and while it happens you are helpless, so the best thing to do is be far away from anyone watching. Afterwards you make a Transference check to see how much of your other self you keep.

As a wolf you hunt and track, and you have to eat once a week. As a human you learn language, and you don't need to eat at all. Over the course of a game you pick up intangible things, such as social cues, a bigger vocabulary and maybe even letters, but to learn them you have to try them, and your first attempts will mostly fail. Failure is part of the story: six failures in total, or three successes in a row, cement a skill.

Random tables give you a month, day and moon phase, and a place and time to be human in: aboard the Titanic, modern Tokyo, ancient England as the Druids are building Stonehenge, or Sarajevo on the eve of the assassination of Archduke Ferdinand.


Where it is now

Where Wolf? is a work in progress.
