Lopsided Games



Lopsided Games is where I put my small tabletop and party games.


Squirrel Scramble

A board game for 2 to 6 players. You are a squirrel gathering acorns for the winter, dodging dogs and crossing roads. Forthcoming.


Chicken Coop

A cooperative game for 2 to 6 players in which you are all chickens trying to escape the evil chicken farmer. It is more about problem solving than combat, and it needs someone old enough to keep the table from exploding. It might turn out to be a one-page RPG instead. An idea so far.


Runaway Rainbow

A simple party game. You are a leprechaun who has lost their rainbow, so you can't get back to your pot of gold. Spin a wheel to decide which colour you need next, then complete a challenge in real life to win it. Collect all seven colours and travel home along your new rainbow. An idea so far.
