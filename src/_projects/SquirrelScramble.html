Squirrel Scramble



Squirrel Scramble is a board game for 2 to 6 players, ages 7 and up, from Lopsided Games. You are a squirrel trying to gather enough acorns to put away for the coming winter. Scramble across town to the parks, grab some acorns and make it back to your tree before the other squirrels do.


How it plays

On your turn you roll the movement dice and move that many squares across the map. Roads are risky: to cross one you roll the road dice, and if it shows a symbol you have to wait until next turn. Dogs roam the town, and you have to keep a square away from them. Reach a park and you can search it for acorns on the same turn, with bigger parks giving you more chances. But acorns only count once you have carried them home to stash them, and you can't visit the same park twice in a row.

The first squirrel to secure the number of acorns needed to survive the winter wins. That number depends on how many people are playing, and you can lower it for a shorter game.


Where it is now

Squirrel Scramble is a forthcoming game, and I'm playtesting it with a printed map, 3D printed squirrels and homemade tokens.
