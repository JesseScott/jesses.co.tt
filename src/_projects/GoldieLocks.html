Goldie Locks



Goldie Locks is a one-shot adventure for the tabletop RPG Magical Kitties Save the Day. It began as the project for a course in writing your first adventure, and it is about people being easily misunderstood, with a lot of honey along the way.

It is in the works: the writing is done and heading for a final proofread, and the maps, handouts and cover art are being drawn. When it is finished it will be a PDF on DriveThruRPG.
